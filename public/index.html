04 Approach

How I work.

  • Secure by default

    Identity comes first. Least privilege and Conditional Access are part of the design, never a retrofit.

  • Automate the repeatable

    If a task happens twice, it gets scripted. Automation keeps an estate consistent and frees people for real work.

  • Measure what matters

    Good telemetry turns tenant noise into signals people can act on, before users notice anything is wrong.

  • Design for people

    The best security is the kind users barely feel. Every control is weighed against the experience it creates.
